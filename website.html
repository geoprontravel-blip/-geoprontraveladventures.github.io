6 DAYS

Marangu Route – 6 Days

A classic Kilimanjaro adventure through rainforest, moorland and alpine landscapes, finishing with the unforgettable summit experience at Uhuru Peak.

View Full Itinerary & Details

Overview

The Marangu Route is one of the most established routes on Mount Kilimanjaro. It is known for its mountain hut accommodation and beautiful changing landscapes. This itinerary allows time for acclimatization before the summit attempt.

Highlights

  • Marangu Gate
  • Beautiful rainforest
  • Mandara and Horombo Huts
  • Kibo alpine desert
  • Uhuru Peak – Roof of Africa
  • Sunrise from the summit area

Day 1 – Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut

Transfer to Marangu Gate for registration and begin the trek through lush rainforest to Mandara Hut.

Day 2 – Mandara Hut to Horombo Hut

Continue through the forest and into the moorland zone with views of Kibo and Mawenzi. Overnight at Horombo Hut.

Day 3 – Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut

Trek across the alpine desert toward Kibo. The landscape becomes increasingly dry as you approach the summit zone.

Day 4 – Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak to Horombo Hut

Begin the summit attempt during the night. Reach Uhuru Peak, the highest point in Africa, then descend to Horombo Hut.

Day 5 – Horombo Hut to Mandara Hut

Descend through the moorland and rainforest to Mandara Hut.

Day 6 – Mandara Hut to Marangu Gate

Complete the descent through the rainforest and return to Marangu Gate. Transfer back to Moshi.

A scenic and rewarding route offering varied landscapes, excellent acclimatization and an unforgettable summit experience.

View Full Itinerary & Details

Overview

The Machame Route is famous for its dramatic scenery and varied terrain. The seven-day itinerary provides additional time for acclimatization and a gradual approach to the summit.

Highlights

  • Shira Plateau
  • Lava Tower
  • Barranco Wall
  • Karanga Valley
  • Uhuru Peak

Day 1 – Machame Gate to Machame Camp

Registration at Machame Gate followed by a trek through the rainforest to Machame Camp.

Day 2 – Machame Camp to Shira Camp

Leave the rainforest and climb toward the Shira Plateau with spectacular mountain views.

Day 3 – Shira Camp to Lava Tower to Barranco Camp

Trek toward Lava Tower for acclimatization before descending into the Barranco Valley.

Day 4 – Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp

Climb the famous Barranco Wall and continue through the alpine landscape to Karanga.

Day 5 – Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp

Continue toward Barafu Camp, the base for the summit attempt.

Day 6 – Barafu Camp to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

Begin the summit attempt during the night, reach Uhuru Peak and descend toward Mweka Camp.

Day 7 – Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate

Complete the descent through the rainforest and transfer back to Moshi.

Day 3 – Shira 1 to Shira 2

Explore the high-altitude plateau while continuing the acclimatization process.

Day 4 – Shira 2 to Lava Tower to Barranco Camp

Day 5 – Barranco to Karanga Camp

Climb the Barranco Wall and continue toward Karanga Valley.

Day 6 – Karanga to Barafu Camp

Continue toward Barafu Camp, preparing for the summit attempt.

Day 7 – Barafu to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

Summit attempt during the night, reach Uhuru Peak and descend toward Mweka Camp.

Day 8 – Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate

Northern Circuit – 9 Days

A longer and quieter route around Kilimanjaro offering spectacular scenery and extensive acclimatization before the summit.

View Full Itinerary & Details

Overview

The Northern Circuit is the longest main trekking route on Kilimanjaro. It travels around the quieter northern side of the mountain before joining the summit approach.

Highlights

  • Remote northern slopes
  • Shira Plateau
  • Moir Hut area
  • Pofu and Third Cave areas
  • Kibo summit zone
  • Uhuru Peak

Day 1 – Londorossi Gate to Mti Mkubwa

Registration and trek through the rainforest to Mti Mkubwa Camp.

Day 2 – Mti Mkubwa to Shira Camp

Continue through the forest toward the open landscapes of the Shira Plateau.

Day 3 – Shira Camp to Moir Hut

Cross the plateau and continue toward the Moir area.

Day 4 – Moir Hut to Pofu Camp

Trek through the northern slopes while acclimatizing to increasing altitude.

Day 5 – Pofu Camp to Third Cave Camp

Continue around the northern circuit toward Third Cave.

Day 6 – Third Cave to School Hut

Continue toward the eastern summit approach and School Hut.

Day 7 – School Hut to Uhuru Peak to Horombo

Summit attempt followed by descent toward Horombo.

Day 8 – Horombo to Mandara

Descend through the moorland toward Mandara Hut.

Day 9 – Mandara to Marangu Gate

Complete the descent through the rainforest and transfer to Moshi.
